    Zoom out without switching apps.

    The daily page is where you write. Everything else is the same days, seen from further away.

    1. A day

      Notes, habits, time, reflexion, learnings and a one-line recap. Plus recurring checklists that reset every morning — the shutdown routine, the gym bag.

    2. A week

      Weekly notes and a week reflexion, starting Monday. Open while the week runs, then kept as it was.

    3. A cycle

      A stretch of weeks you name yourself, like a semester or a quarter. Set its priorities, write under each one, and add goals that count themselves from your habits or logged time.

    4. A year

      Yearly goals, as a yes or no or a number to reach. The Life calendar with every recap. A learning log that keeps growing.

    Goals that keep score on their own.

    Link a cycle goal to a habit or a project and it fills in as you live the days. Tick off a workout, the goal moves. Stop a timer, the hours count. No progress numbers to remember to update on Sunday night.

    Yearly goals sit on their own page, apart from any cycle, so the big things stay visible all year.

    A record that stays a record.

    A journal is only worth rereading if it says what you thought at the time. So there are a few simple rules about what can be changed, and the server enforces them — not just the screen.

    Yesterday is still open.
    Forgot to log last night? Any past day can be filled in or corrected.
    Tomorrow stays blank.
    Future days can’t be written ahead. A page starts when its day does.
    A week closes on Sunday.
    Weekly notes and the week reflexion are editable during that week only.
    A cycle ends on its end date.
    After that, its priorities and notes stay exactly as you left them.

      Quick when you know where you’re going.

      Press ⌘K anywhere in the app to jump to a view, a habit, a project or a cycle. Type / in any note for a checkbox or a heading. Try the box on the left — it filters the same commands.

      Private by construction.

      What you write about your days is nobody else’s business. That’s built into how the data is stored, not left to good intentions.

      Isolated in the database

      Every row carries its owner, and Postgres row-level security refuses any read or write by another account — even if the app itself had a bug.

      Export everything, any time

      One click downloads all of your data as JSON: every day, habit, cycle, goal and time entry.

      Delete means deleted

      Deleting your account removes it and everything attached to it, permanently. Nothing is kept on the side.

      Questions

      What does it cost?

      Nothing. Sign up with an email and a password and today’s page is ready.

      Do I have to use every feature?

      No. Every tab and every view is optional. Plenty of people only write notes and a recap; habits, time and cycles are there when you want them.

      Can I go back and change an old entry?

      Daily pages, yes — any day up to today. Weekly notes only during their week, and a cycle until its end date. Future days can’t be written ahead.

      When does a new day start?

      At midnight, Berlin time. The date is decided on the server, so it’s the same on every device you use.

      Can I take my data with me?

      Yes. Account settings has a JSON export of everything you’ve written and tracked, available whenever you want it.

      I forgot my password.

      Use “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen and you’ll get a reset link by email.
